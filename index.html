How it works
  • Controls: tap / click / Space = flap. E or ⚡ = hero power. P/Esc = pause.
  • Pass through the gap in each red banner. Hit a banner or the road = show over.
  • Seats: +1 per banner, +3 CENTER if you pass near the middle, +2 WHISTLE if you scrape the edge.
  • Combo: every bonus pass in a row adds +1 extra seat per step.
